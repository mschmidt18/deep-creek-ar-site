How it works

  1. 01 — POINT

    Aim the camera at a lakefront house

    From the boat, the dock, or the road. GPS and compass decide what's in front of you — no scanning, no tapping around a map.

  2. 02 — SEE

    The property is labeled in place

    Agency name and distance, drawn over the live camera view. Built for moving boats: sensor-based AR that doesn't depend on world tracking.

  3. 03 — TAP

    Open the agency's own listing

    Every property links straight to the rental agency's website for photos, availability, and booking. The app sells nothing.

Made for spotty lake service

The full property database ships inside the app, so it works with no signal mid-lake. When you're back online, the app quietly picks up the latest data from this site.
